# Ixion

Declination: -3°36'24"
Interpretation: Ixion represents the consequences of betrayal, lawlessness, and the corruption that comes from unchecked desire. Named after the first murderer in Greek mythology who was bound to a fiery wheel in Tartarus, this Plutino embodies lust for life, ambition without ethics, and the karmic repercussions of transgression. Ixion shows where we must develop discernment about our desires and values, recognizing that creative life force can become destructive when misdirected. This placement indicates the capacity for passionate intensity, but warns against tyranny, criminality, and the violation of sacred trust. Ixion teaches that our actions create karmic patterns that eventually reflect back to us, demanding accountability and ethical awareness.
