Eros represents the immense primordial force of desire for manifestation and experience, which informs the entire Cosmos.  It shows dynamic energy, passion and will-to-power, including sexual passion and attraction.  Eros's symbolism includes the idea of 'passion' as suffering.  There is a fiery, burning quality to the energy of Eros, which sometimes runs out of control and can lead to scorching on many levels.  When the fires of desire go wrong, there can be a lot of suffering and damage.
