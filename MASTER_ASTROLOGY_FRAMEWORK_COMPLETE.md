## Essential Dignity Scoring System

### Dignity Hierarchy & Point Values

The essential dignity system measures a planet's fundamental strength and authority within a sign. Each dignity level carries specific point values that accumulate to create a comprehensive strength assessment.

| Dignity Type | Points | Description | Manifestation |
|--------------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Domicile (Rulership)** | +5 | Planet in its home sign; maximum authority and resource access | Full expression of planetary nature; natural ease and competence |
| **Exaltation** | +4 | Planet in sign of highest respect; amplified influence and honor | Heightened expression; exceptional capability; natural advantage |
| **Triplicity** | +3 | Planet in sign of same element; general community support | Elemental harmony; comfortable operation; natural affinity |
| **Term (Bounds)** | +2 | Planet within specific degree range of sign; local jurisdiction | Limited but recognized authority; contextual power |
| **Face (Decanate)** | +1 | Planet in 10-degree section; minimal influence | Weakest dignity; marginal support; requires other factors |
| **Peregrine** | 0 | Planet with no essential dignities; dependent on dispositor | No inherent authority; relies entirely on dispositor's condition |
| **Detriment (Exile)** | -4 | Planet opposite its rulership; conflicted operation; lack of resources | Difficulty expressing nature; requires conscious effort; external obstacles |
| **Fall** | -5 | Planet opposite its exaltation; depressed function; inherent weakness | Struggle with expression; natural disadvantage; internal obstacles |

## Accidental Strength Modifiers

Accidental strength refers to factors that modify a planet's essential dignity through placement, motion, and relationship to other chart factors.

### 1. House Placement Strength

| House Category | Houses | Strength | Effect |
|----------------|--------|----------|--------|
| **Angular** | 1, 4, 7, 10 | +3 | Accidentally strong; visible, tangible events; direct manifestation |
| **Succedent** | 2, 5, 8, 11 | +1 | Moderate influence; developing situations; gradual manifestation |
| **Cadent** | 3, 6, 9, 12 | -1 | Accidentally weak; internal/preparatory; diminishing influence |

### 2. Planetary Motion States

| Motion State | Strength Modifier | Interpretation |
