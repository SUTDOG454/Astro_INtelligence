Hephaistos is named for the ancient fire-god and blacksmith, the master craftsman of Olympus (the Roman equivalent was Vulcan).  It represents inner fire and creative passions, manual dexterity, willingness to work, and often a sense of isolation and rejection.   In astrological terms, Hephaistos like Astraea seems to be important when energies either come together strongly like a metal alloy, or blow apart mightily, like a volcanic eruption.  There seems to be an intense physicality with this symbol, and just as the mythical Hephaistos had a limp, there can be physical disabilities or a sense of being 'different' or of somehow disliking one's appearance if this asteroid is strong, or linked to the Ascendant.  Hephaistos people think and feel things very deeply, sometimes too deeply, and sometimes feel awkward or misunderstood or rejected.  Social skills are not always well developed, and Hephaistos does seem to show up strongly amongst those who suffer from depressive illnesses.
