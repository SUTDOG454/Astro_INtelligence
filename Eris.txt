Eris is named for the ancient Greek goddess of strife and discord.  The discovery of this 'scattered disc object' upset the world of astronomy and forced a re-think of how astronomers define a 'planet', leading to the invention of the category of 'dwarf planet' which now includes Pluto and Ceres and Eris, and the new objects Haumea and Make-Make.  Eris needs substantial research, but early signs suggest it is not an intrinsically malign influence in the horoscope, regardless of the dire mythology.  Instead, Eris seems to be a force for evolutionary movement, an agent of change – and then some!  Eris moves incredibly slowly across the zodiac, but its movement in terms of influence (on character, on events) is fast, sometimes 'fast and furious'.  There is impatience with this symbol, a sense of being driven to do something.  What someone then does may or may not prove to be clever or useful, but at least Eris sees to it that something is happening.  Eris goads us forward, like it or not.
