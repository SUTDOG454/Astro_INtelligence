## Philosophical Foundations

### 1. Arroyo Philosophy: Energy Language

**Core Principle:** Astrology as a language of energy focusing on inner experience and psychological dynamics.

**Key Principles:**

- **Astrology is a language of energy:** Learn to speak it fluently to understand psychological patterns
- **Focus on essentials:** Ignore minor factors that confuse rather than clarify
- **Prioritize inner experience:** Charts show psychological dynamics more than external events
- **Use guidelines, not rules:** Adapt each interpretation to the unique person
- **Think wholistically:** Synthesis is more valuable than analysis

**Application:** This philosophy emphasizes understanding the native's subjective experience, emotional patterns, and psychological growth. It rejects deterministic interpretations in favor of empowering guidance that respects individual agency.

### 2. Magi Philosophy: Cosmic Sympathy

**Core Principle:** The celestial pattern is resonant, not causative—a higher-dimensional signature manifesting through all scales.

**Key Principles:**

**Cosmic Sympathy**
- The celestial pattern is resonant, not causative
- A higher-dimensional signature manifesting through all scales
- Corollary: "As above, so below; as within, so without"
- Implications: Synchronicity, fractal resonance, pattern recognition

**Anima Mundi Participation**
- Planets as concentrations of archetypal consciousness
- One can engage dialogically with planetary intelligences
- Corollary: "All is ensouled; consciousness pervades cosmos"
- Implications: Planetary intelligences, symbolic perception, participatory cosmology

**Telesmatic Imagination**
- Astrological symbols as gateways to direct experiential knowing
- Through visualization and embodiment
- Corollary: "To know is to become"
- Implications: Ritual magic, pathworking, active imagination

**Application:** This philosophy treats astrology as a sacred technology for consciousness expansion, not merely personality analysis. It emphasizes direct engagement with archetypal energies through imagination and ritual.

### 3. Traditional Philosophy: Sacred Divination

**Core Principle:** Astrology as a sacred language of divination—a way to understand the Divine Mind.

**Cosmology:** Geocentric model with Earth as the center of divine observation

**Hermetic Influence:** Rooted in Hermetic principles of "as above, so below"

**Divine Order:** Astrology represents humanity's attempt to understand the Will of God or Divine Plan

**Application:** This perspective views astrology as a spiritual practice connecting human consciousness to cosmic order. It emphasizes the sacred dimension of astrological knowledge and its role in understanding divine intention.

### 4. Transits Philosophy: Temporal Dynamics

**What Are Transits?**
Ongoing movement of planets compared to their positions at birth. Transits reflect both collective reality and personal triggers for change.

**Natal Chart:** Snapshot of the sky at birth representing the unchanging core self and potential

**Midpoints:** Sensitive points calculated as the exact middle between two planets or points. They represent the integration of archetypal energies.

**Archetypal Pairs:** Divine couples representing cosmic dualities and integration principles

**Application:** Transits provide the temporal dimension to natal chart interpretation, showing how archetypal energies activate and evolve throughout life.

## Cosmological Perspectives

### Geocentric Cosmology

**Purpose:** Earth-centered perspective showing personal experience and manifestation

**Use Cases:**
- Natal analysis and personality assessment
- Psychological insight and emotional patterns
- Mundane events and daily life circumstances

**Considered Real:** Phenomenological reality—what is directly experienced

**Interpretation:** The geocentric chart represents the native's subjective experience of reality. It shows how planetary energies manifest in personal life and consciousness.

### Heliocentric Cosmology

**Purpose:** Sun-centered perspective showing soul's purpose and cosmic alignment

**Use Cases:**
- Karmic patterns and soul evolution
- Super aspects and higher-dimensional connections
- Spiritual purpose and cosmic alignment

**Considered Real:** Archetypal reality—the realm of pure principles and forms

**Interpretation:** The heliocentric chart reveals the soul's deeper purpose and its alignment with cosmic principles. It shows patterns that transcend personal psychology.

### Draconic Cosmology

**Purpose:** Node-centered perspective showing karmic blueprint and soul's journey

**Method:** Subtract natal North Node position from all chart points

**Use Cases:**
- Past life patterns and soul contracts
- Karmic obligations and dharmic purpose
- Soul's evolutionary intention

**Considered Real:** Causal reality—the realm of karma and soul contracts

**Interpretation:** The draconic chart reveals the soul's karmic blueprint and evolutionary intention. It shows what the soul came to learn and accomplish.

## Chart Construction & Methodology

### Required Data for Chart Calculation

**Natal Chart Data:**
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD format
- **Time:** HH:MM:SS (24-hour format)
- **Place:** City, country, latitude, longitude, timezone (IANA format preferred)

**Event Chart Data:**
- **For Electional:** Same structure as natal
- **For Horary:** Time question is asked

### Chart Types & House Systems

**Geocentric Charts (Tropical Zodiac)**

| House System | Use Case | Description | Best For |
|--------------|----------|-------------|----------|
| Placidus | Standard Magi method | Most common in traditional Magi astrology | General analysis |
| Campanus | Tracy Marks system | Recommended in "The Art of Chart Interpretation" | Detailed analysis |
| Porphyry | Elemental balance | Equal division of quadrants | Elemental emphasis |
| Whole Sign | Simplicity | Each sign = one house | Planetary joys |
| Alcabitius | Magical timing | Used for planetary hour calculations | Electional astrology |

**Planets Included in Standard Analysis:**
- Personal planets: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars
- Social planets: Jupiter, Saturn
- Outer planets: Uranus, Neptune, Pluto
- Sensitive points: Ascendant, Midheaven, Vertex, Chiron

**Aspect Orbs (Degrees of Influence):**

| Aspect | Orb | Interpretation |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Conjunction | 8° | Fusion, integration |
| Opposition | 8° | Polarity, tension |
| Square | 8° | Challenge, growth |
| Trine | 8° | Harmony, ease |
| Sextile | 6° | Support, opportunity |
| Quincunx | 3° | Adjustment, refinement |
| Semi-sextile | 2° | Subtle connection |
| Semi-square | 2° | Minor tension |

### Heliocentric Charts

**Zodiac:** Tropical (same as geocentric)  
**Origin:** Sun as center  
**Planets Included:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Chiron  
**Special Note:** Moon is not included; Earth becomes a "planet" in heliocentric perspective

### Draconic Charts

**Method:** Subtract natal North Node position from all points  
**Zodiac Start:** 0° Aries at North Node  
**Purpose:** Soul's karmic blueprint and evolutionary intention

### Super Aspects

**Definition:** Aspects between geocentric and heliocentric positions

**Calculation Method:** Compare each geocentric planet with each heliocentric planet

**Significance Levels:**

| Level | Name | Orb | Significance |
|-------|------|-----|--------------|
| 1 | Minor Super Aspect | 1.0° | Noticeable but not life-defining |
| 2 | Major Super Aspect | 0.5° | Life-shaping patterns |
| 3 | Perfect Super Aspect | 0.1° | Fated, karmically significant |
