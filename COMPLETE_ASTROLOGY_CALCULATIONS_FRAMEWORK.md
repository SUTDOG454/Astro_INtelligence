### Aspect Orb Guidelines

**Orb Strength Rules:**
- **Tight (≤1°):** Maximum intensity; precise timing; immediate manifestation
- **Medium (1-2°):** High intensity; strong influence; clear manifestation
- **Standard (2-4°):** Moderate intensity; normal influence; noticeable effects
- **Wide (4-8°):** Lower intensity; background influence; gradual effects
- **Very Wide (>8°):** Minimal intensity; subtle influence; barely perceptible

**Luminaries (Sun/Moon) Orbs:** ±8° (wider orbs allowed)  
**Personal Planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars) Orbs:** ±4-6°  
**Social Planets (Jupiter, Saturn) Orbs:** ±4-6°  
**Outer Planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) Orbs:** ±2-4°

## Donna Cunningham's Chart Delineation Framework

### Systematic Interpretation Process

Donna Cunningham's approach emphasizes systematic, thorough analysis following a specific sequence:

**Stage 1: Chart Overview (Visual Assessment)**
- Examine overall chart shape and distribution
- Identify hemisphere emphasis (upper/lower, left/right)
- Note immediate standouts (stelliums, singletons, unaspected planets)
- Assess overall aspect character (harmonious, challenging, mixed)

**Stage 2: Big Three Analysis**
- **Sun:** Core identity, conscious will, life purpose
- **Moon:** Emotional nature, instinctive responses, needs
- **Ascendant:** Personality mask, first impressions, life approach

**Stage 3: Chart Ruler Analysis**
- Identify planet ruling Ascendant sign
- Analyze chart ruler's sign, house, aspects, dignity
- Recognize chart ruler as most important single planet
- Understand how chart ruler colors all other planetary expressions

**Stage 4: Personal Planets**
- Mercury: Communication style, thinking process
- Venus: Values, relationship needs, aesthetic sense
- Mars: Drive, assertion style, sexual expression
- Jupiter: Expansion areas, belief systems, luck
- Saturn: Limitations, lessons, mastery areas

**Stage 5: House Analysis**
- Identify which houses contain planets
- Interpret empty houses through their rulers
- Recognize house emphasis patterns
- Understand life area focus

**Stage 6: Aspect Analysis**
- Map all major aspects
- Identify aspect patterns (Grand Cross, T-Square, Grand Trine, Kite, Yod)
- Assess overall aspect character
- Recognize tightest and most influential aspects

**Stage 7: Outer Planets**
- Uranus: Innovation, rebellion, uniqueness
- Neptune: Spirituality, illusion, transcendence
- Pluto: Transformation, power, psychology

**Stage 8: Nodal Axis**
- North Node: Soul's growth direction
- South Node: Innate gifts and past patterns
- Evolutionary intention and karmic purpose

**Stage 9: Synthesis**
- Integrate all findings into coherent narrative
- Identify major life themes
- Provide empowering interpretation
- Offer practical guidance

## Magi Astrology Encyclopedia

### Core Magi Principles

**Cosmic Sympathy:** "As above, so below" — celestial patterns resonate through all scales of reality.

**Anima Mundi:** All is ensouled consciousness; the universe is alive and intelligent.

**Telesmatic Imagination:** Symbols are gateways to direct engagement with archetypal energies.

**Midpoints:** Integration points where two archetypal energies merge and create new meaning.

**Super Aspects:** Heliocentric aspects revealing soul-level patterns beyond personal manifestation.

### Magi Techniques

**1. Midpoint Analysis**
- Calculate midpoint between two planets
- Interpret as integration of both planetary energies
- Use for deeper understanding of complex dynamics
- Particularly powerful in synastry and transit work

**2. Planetary Pairs**
- Sun-Moon: Conscious and unconscious integration
- Mercury-Venus: Communication and values
- Mars-Venus: Desire and love
- Jupiter-Saturn: Growth and structure
- Uranus-Neptune: Innovation and transcendence

**3. Heliocentric Charts**
- Use Sun as center rather than Earth
- Reveals soul-level patterns
- Shows karmic intentions
- Complements geocentric for complete picture

**4. Super Aspects**
- Aspects visible only in heliocentric perspective
- Indicate soul-level agreements and patterns
- Particularly significant in synastry
- Show karmic connections
