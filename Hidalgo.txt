Hidalgo seems to balance Sappho.  Another symbol like Mars, Hidalgo is assertive, and associated with a sense of privilege and the drive to demonstrate excellence and force in the world.  The name Hidalgo comes from the Spanish, where hidalgo is a title, denoting the lowest rank of hereditary nobility.  It means, in effect, that any man who is entitled to be called hidalgo is a gentleman, a cut above the common herd.  From this we can discern an astrological association with ideas of entitlement, and of a masculine energy that seeks to demonstrate superiority in ways that range from the skilful and admirable to the unskilful and unpleasant or foolish.   Demetra George also associates this asteroid with revolutionary vigour and with the urge to either dominate or protect.
