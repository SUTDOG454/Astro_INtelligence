Hekate is named for the Greek goddess of the old moon, of midnight and the cross-roads, later demonized as the leader of witches.  Her energy seems to balance that of the North Node, both in terms of the lunar association and in terms of the sheer power Hekate manifests, which has some similarities with that of Rahu. There is 'magic' power here, ultimately derived from deep powers of concentration and manifestation.  Hekate relates to the gifts of deep experience, and can be a marker of wealth, especially the material kind.  The power constellated by Hekate also seems to have a distinctly sexy quality: this asteroid can be an important marker of physical attraction when considering chart synastry.
