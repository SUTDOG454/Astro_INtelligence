Ganesa is named for Ganesha, one of the most popular gods of the Hindu pantheon, associated therein with the planet Jupiter.  The god is a patron of the arts and sciences, and is also honoured as the remover of obstacles, although he is also credited with creating obstacles from time to time.  The asteroid Ganesa needs much more research, but early signs are that it has tremendous power in the chart, and it should not be ignored.  It seems to be a symbol of the organizing power of consciousness and the skill or lack thereof we each bring to our lives in this regard.  The closest aspects made by Ganesa can be a swift shortcut to understanding where the blockages or obstacles are both in the psyche and in the material life.  Those energies meshing with the energy of Ganesa seem either to flow very well or fail to flow at all helpfully; instead they can go seriously awry.  Most of the time there is work to do wherever Ganesa goes, a work involving deep self-knowledge, a willingness to face the temptations of drifting or doing nothing, and a willingness to utilize whatever strategies will set one free from any kind of bondage, especially the bondage of the ego.
