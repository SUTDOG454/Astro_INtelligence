Gilgamesh is named for an actual king of ancient Sumer, whose exploits passed into myth and who was later considered to have been deified.  Gilgamesh is the mythological prototype for the superhero figures of Herakles and Hercules.  The asteroid with this name also constellates themes around the drive to be and do the best, to demonstrate physical or moral strength and superiority (or both), and to make maximum exuberant use of all talents available.  The unskilful expressions of this asteroid show as conceit, overweening pride and arrogance, bullying and the ruthless use of other people and/or their resources.  Skilful expressions of Gilgamesh show as deep courage (both physical and moral), the willingness to fight for the right, the understanding that the strong have a duty to protect and help the weak, and the capacity to act as a guide or mentor to help weaker or less confident people build up their strength and skills.  Gilgamesh people can be very resolute and bold.  Unless there are many introverted or under-confident influences elsewhere, they tend to come on strong, if necessary come out fighting, and have in some sense the attributes of the 'spiritual warrior' at their command.
