Hebe is named for the goddess of youth.  The asteroid symbolism does relate to young people, young women and girls in particular, but also covers themes of service and servitude, the willingness to help people and the potential for that impulse to be exploited, and at the highest expression, an ardour to serve in a spiritual sense, which reaches culmination in the urge for actual union with the Divine.  Hebe people are often very intense emotionally, or find themselves entangled in intense emotional experiences.  These are not always pleasant.  One of the important themes with this asteroid seems to be around the claiming of selfhood and autonomy, the cultivation of the talents needed for self-actualization, or from the very highest spiritual perspective, for Self-realization.
