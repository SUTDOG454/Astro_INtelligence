Iris is named for the goddess messenger of Hera (or Juno, as the Romans called her, whose symbol is the rainbow.  It is very important as a symbol of the activity of the mind, and I have come to see Iris as symbolizing the yin 'right brain' mode of thinking - intuitive, imaginative and receptive - to balance the yang 'left brain' functions of systems thinking, data gathering and analysis which seem more Mercurial.  Iris also has some bearing on what is fashionably called 'emotional intelligence'.  Its energy is of particular value in raising the level of overall intelligence, and it shows with vivid colour the gifts of the imagination.  Another great Iridian gift is the capacity to listen to others, and it tends to show enhanced empathy.  Where Iris energy is blunted or distorted these gifts may be impaired, too, of course: in synastry Iris can show where there is sympathy and a willingness to listen and to receive the other person, or where that capacity has shut down or never really activated in the first place.  Iris tends to tell the truth, where Mercury is traditionally the symbol of the trickster who tells lies.  Iris people can be great talkers or writers, and in more unskilful expressions, they can be quite sweetly self-obsessed and unaware of the world around them.  This is a distortion of the better Iridian gifts: holistic awareness, and a generous willingness to give other people their space and their say.  Iris is hugely important as a symbol of light, both in physical terms and in many metaphorical and metaphysical ways, as Iris seems to be the great symbol of the Light of Consciousness.  It can give great spiritual aspiration and enhanced spiritual gifts, and seems to be the primary symbol for real meditation practices and their blessings.
