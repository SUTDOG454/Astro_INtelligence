Harmonia is named for the goddess of harmony, in myth the daughter of Aphrodite (Venus) and many possible fathers depending on the story, including Ares (Mars).  As the personification of the exalted force of harmony, and described as the goddess of unity, Harmonia is a particularly beautiful and gracious deity, and her asteroid seems to constellate all of her lovely qualities.  In the horoscope, Harmonia points to where we can express or find harmony and concord.  It seems to be an important symbol in synastry, as a marker for mutual attraction and support, and the ability to get along with other people and resolve conflicts.
