Flora is named for the Roman goddess of spring, and flowers.  It represents a manifestation of creative energy:  that which is coming into bloom, flourishing, anything 'whose time has come'.  Flora seems to have the potential to bestow some measure of grace and happiness, and works well as a shakti or polarity partner for Bacchus.  The energy of this asteroid is very beautiful (for the most part) and also very strong – not in an extroverted or macho way, but manifesting instead as a kind of grace, the 'soft power' that helps to resolve deadlocks and move situations forward in a way that makes everyone feel a winner.  Flora is a very important symbol in synastry, prospering both attraction and the ability to help the partner (or significant other of any kind) to flower, to reach full potential.  In mundane astrology Flora's interactions with other asteroids and planets are very important for showing whatever energy is at that time blossoming and able to express in a full, strong, confident way.
