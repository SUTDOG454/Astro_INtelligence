Herculina is an asteroid whose name is of uncertain origin, but it appears to be the feminine form of Hercules.  Certainly the themes which show with this asteroid are relevant to the idea (and ideal) of the superhero and the quest to demonstrate excellence or to assert dominance.  Herculina is a symbol of power and strength wherever it goes, but that strength needs to be carefully expressed or it can manifest as bumptiousness, and alienate people.  A positive sense of self-worth is the skilful expression of Herculina, whilst unskilful expressions show as arrogance or blustering or even bullying.  The discovery chart for this asteroid suggests that there are important themes around the mind, too, and there is an interesting feature with the zodiacal positions of Herculina, at least for the period during which it is possible to find this asteroid (after 1500 CE).  Herculina seems to keep pace fairly closely with the asteroid Pallas, is often conjunct or within one sign, and never appears to be more than about seventy degrees away from Pallas.  Whether they are perpetually linked like this because of orbital peculiarities or whether they have a long cycle of conjunctions, is not yet clear.
