# Astrology Framework Research Notes

## Traditional Chart Delineation Methods

### Overall Natal Chart Delineation Process

**Step 1: Determine Sect (Diurnal or Nocturnal)**
- Identify in-sect benefic and malefic (especially helpful/protective)
- Identify out-of-sect benefic and malefic (out-of-sect malefic is major source of difficulties)

**Step 2: Evaluate Sect Light and Trigon Lords**
- Assess their condition and placement
- Used to evaluate overall fortune of native's life

**Step 3: Evaluate Each Planet Individually**
- In sect or out
- Benefic or malefic nature
- Rulerships (domicile/exaltation/triplicity/bound/face)
- Check planet's domicile ruler and its condition
- Direct or retrograde
- House placement (angular/succedent/cadent)
- Other planets in same sign
- Mutual receptions
- Houses ruled and whether planet "sees" its houses by whole sign aspect
- Planets in stakes (oppositions/squares, especially malefics)
- Heliacal rising or setting (within 7 days of birth)

**Step 4: Check Chart Quality**
- Angles: cardinal, fixed, or mutable
- Planets: same quality check
- Predominance of single quality reflects overall pattern

**Step 5: Ascendant Analysis**
- Lord of Ascendant location and condition
- Lord of the Ascendant lord

**Step 6: Special Features**
- Part of Fortune and its Lord
- Temperament evaluation
- Mercury and Moon condition (for character and mind)

### Delineation of Specific Life Areas

For any subject consider:
- Natural ruler
- House for the subject
- Planets in the house and their condition
- Lord of the house and its condition
- Any Lot associated with that subject

### Timing Techniques (Prediction)

Techniques include:
- Profections (annual and smaller increments)
- Solar Revolutions
- Distribution through the bounds
- Primary directions
- Transits (only within context of above techniques)

Goal: Find the **Time Lords** - planets activated and in charge of specific subjects at specific periods

## Planetary Strength Calculation Systems

### 1. Western Traditional System (Lilly's Dignity Scoring)

**Score Range:** +38 (exceedingly dignified) to -38 (severely debilitated)

**Concept:** The **Almuten** (Lord of the Geniture) - the planet with highest score, most eminent in the chart

#### Essential Dignities (Positive)
- Own sign or mutual reception by sign: +5
- Exaltation or mutual reception by exaltation: +4
- Own triplicity: +3
- Own terms: +2
- Own face: +1

#### Essential Debilities (Negative)
- In Detriment: -5
- In Fall: -4
- Peregrine (no essential dignity): -5

#### Accidental Dignities (Positive)
- In 10th or 1st house: +5
- In 7th, 4th, or 11th house: +4
- In 2nd or 5th house: +3
- In 9th house: +2
- In 3rd house: +1
- Direct in motion: +4
- Swift in motion: +2
- Saturn/Jupiter/Mars when oriental: +2
- Mercury/Venus when occidental: +2
- Moon increasing in light (waxing): +2
- Free from combustion and Sun's beams: +5
- Cazimi (within 0°17' of Sun): +5
- Partile conjunction with Jupiter or Venus: +5
- Partile conjunction with North Node: +4
- Partile trine with Jupiter or Venus: +4
- Partile sextile with Jupiter or Venus: +3
- Conjunct fixed star Regulus (29°40 Leo): +6
- Conjunct fixed star Spica (23°40 Libra): +5

#### Accidental Debilities (Negative)
- In 12th house: -5
- In 8th or 6th house: -2
- Retrograde: -5
- Slow in motion: -2
- Saturn/Jupiter/Mars when occidental: -2
- Mercury/Venus when oriental: -2
- Moon decreasing in light (waning): -2
- Combust (within 8°30' of Sun): -5
- Under Sun's beams (within 17° of Sun): -4
- Partile conjunction with Saturn or Mars: -5
- Partile conjunction with South Node: -4
- Besieged of Saturn and Mars: -4
- Partile opposition with Saturn or Mars: -4
- Partile square with Saturn or Mars: -4
- Conjunct fixed star Algol or within 5° (26°04 Taurus): -4

**Notes:**
- Partile aspect = exact or within 1 degree
- Oriental = rises before Sun
- Occidental = rises after Sun
- Not applicable to Sun/Moon for direct/retrograde

### 2. Vedic System (Shadbala - Six-fold Strength)

**Measurement Unit:** Rupa/Virupa (1 Rupa = 60 Virupa)

**Minimum Required Strength (in Virupa):**
- Sun: 390
- Moon: 360
- Mars: 300
- Mercury: 420
- Jupiter: 390
- Venus: 330
- Saturn: 300

#### Six Components of Shadbala:

**1. Sthana Bala (Positional Strength)**
- **Uccha Bala:** Exaltation/debilitation strength (proportional to distance from exaltation/debilitation point)
- **Sapta Vargiya Bala:** Strength in seven divisional charts (Rashi, Hora, Drekkana, Saptamsha, Navamsha, Dwadasamsha, Trimsamsha)
- **Oja-Yugma Rashi-Amsha Bala:** Odd/even sign placement in Rashi and Navamsha
- **Kendradi Bala:** Kendra (1-4-7-10) = full strength, Panapara (2-5-8-11) = half, Apoklimas (12-3-6-9) = quarter
- **Drekkana Bala:** Based on placement in first, second, or third Drekkana

**2. Dig Bala (Directional Strength)**
Full strength in:
- 1st house: Jupiter, Mercury
- 10th house: Sun, Mars
- 4th house: Venus, Moon
- 7th house: Saturn

**3. Kala Bala (Temporal Strength)**
- **Natonnata Bala:** Day/night strength (Moon/Mars/Saturn stronger at night; Sun/Jupiter/Venus stronger during day; Mercury both)
- **Paksha Bala:** Benefics stronger in Shukla Paksha, malefics in Krishna Paksha
- **Tribhagiya Bala:** Strength during different thirds of day/night
- **Varsha-Maasa-Dina-Hora Bala:** Yearly, monthly, daily, hourly lord strength
- **Ayana Bala:** Declination strength (position relative to celestial equator)
- **Yuddha Bala:** Planetary war strength (when planets within each other's orbs)

**4. Chesta Bala (Motional Strength)**
Eight types of motion:
- Vakra (retrograde)
- Anuvakra (entering previous sign retrograde)
- Vikala (no motion)
- Manda (slower than usual)
- Mandatara (even slower)
- Sama (neither fast nor slow)
- Chara (faster than Sama)
- Atichara (entering next sign accelerated)

Note: For Sun, Ayana Bala = Chesta Bala; For Moon, Paksha Bala = Chesta Bala

**5. Naisargika Bala (Natural Strength)**
Fixed natural strength (ascending order):
Saturn < Mars < Mercury < Jupiter < Venus < Moon < Sun

**6. Drik Bala (Aspect Strength)**
- Benefic aspects = strength
- Malefic aspects = weakness
- All planets: 7th aspect (full strength)
- Mars: 4th/8th aspects (3/4 strength)
- Jupiter: 5th/9th aspects (1/2 strength)
- Saturn: 3rd/10th aspects (1/4 strength)

#### Required Component Strengths (in Virupa):

**For Jupiter, Mercury, Sun:**
- Sthana Bala: 165
- Dig Bala: 35
- Kala Bala: 50
- Chesta Bala: 112
- Ayana Bala: 30

**For Moon, Venus:**
- Sthana Bala: 133
- Dig Bala: 50
- Kala Bala: 30
- Chesta Bala: 100
- Ayana Bala: 40

**For Mars, Saturn:**
- Sthana Bala: 96
- Dig Bala: 30
- Kala Bala: 40
- Chesta Bala: 67
- Ayana Bala: 20

## Key Concepts for Framework

### Mutual Reception
- **By Sign:** Planet A in sign ruled by Planet B, while Planet B in sign ruled by Planet A (e.g., Mars in Libra, Venus in Aries)
- **By Exaltation:** Planet A in exaltation sign of Planet B, while Planet B in exaltation sign of Planet A (e.g., Moon in Aries, Sun in Taurus)

### Heliacal Rising/Setting
- **Rising:** Planet reaches 15° from Sun, moving away (positive)
- **Setting:** Planet 15° from Sun, moving toward it (negative)

### Combustion States
- **Combust:** Within 8°30' of Sun
- **Under Sun's Beams:** Within 17° of Sun
- **Cazimi:** Within 0°17' of Sun (highly dignified)

### Besiegement
- Planet separating from Saturn and next applying to Mars (or vice versa)
- Surrounded by hostile forces
