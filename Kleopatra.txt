Kleopatra is named for a queen of ancient Egypt who has also passed into the realm of myth.  This asteroid seems to be a very good polarity partner for Midas.  There is a catalytic change quality with Kleopatra, a tendency to manifest at times of crisis, though whether this asteroid shows an upswing or a downswing in one's affairs can be hard to judge ahead of time.  Like Midas it seems to have a resonance with the skilfulness or lack thereof in making choices.  There appear to be some similarities with Juno, and even at times with Hekate: both of these asteroids relate to goddesses of immense power, and Kleopatra seems to connect with the effort to demonstrate some form of power in the world.  There also seems to be an element of suddenness in the way Kleopatra acts, which makes this asteroid a little on the wild or unpredictable side like Pallas – as though the best an astrologer can say when Kleopatra is coming into prominence either by transit or progression is, 'Expect the unexpected!'  Although more research is needed, Kleopatra does relate to times when things change: if a person is up (and especially if they have become smug or lazy about it) then Kleopatra's influence can cut them down to size, whilst if someone is down, it can raise them up again.
